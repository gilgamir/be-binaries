# Bibliographie

!!! tip "Par où commencer"
    Trois articles pour se lancer :

    - **Peters et al. 2013**, sur 59 Cyg, qui s'appuie en partie sur des spectres amateurs de BeSS ;
    - **Mourard et al. 2015**, sur φ Per, en accès libre ;
    - **Rivinius et al. 2013**, pour tout savoir sur les étoiles Be.

    Les références arXiv sont en accès libre sur [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org).

## Systèmes Be + sdO

**Mourard D. et al. 2015**
:   *Spectral and spatial imaging of the Be+sdO binary φ Persei*, A&A 577, A51.
    [Article en accès libre](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/full_html/2015/05/aa25141-14/aa25141-14.html)

**Peters G. J. et al. 2013**
:   *FUV Detection of the Suspected Subdwarf Companion to the Be Star 59 Cygni*, ApJ 765, 2.
    [arXiv:1301.0257](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.0257)

**Gies D. R. et al. 1998**
:   *Hubble Space Telescope Goddard High Resolution Spectrograph Observations of the Be + sdO Binary φ Persei*, ApJ 493, 440.

**Poeckert R. 1981**
:   *A Spectroscopic Study of the Binary Be Star φ Persei*, PASP 93, 297.

**Klement R. et al. 2022**
:   *Interferometric Detections of sdO Companions Orbiting Three Classical Be Stars*.
    [arXiv:2112.05073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.05073)

**Klement R. et al. 2024**
:   *The CHARA Array Interferometric Program on the Multiplicity of Classical Be Stars: New Detections and Orbits of Stripped Subdwarf Companions*.
    [arXiv:2312.08252](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08252)

**Wang L., Gies D. R., Peters G. J. 2017**
:   *Detection of the Ultraviolet Spectrum of the Hot Subdwarf Companion of 60 Cygni (B1 Ve) from a Survey of IUE Spectra of Be Stars*, ApJ 843, 60.

**Wang L. et al. 2021**
:   *The Detection and Characterization of Be+sdO Binaries from HST/STIS FUV Spectroscopy*, AJ 161, 248.

    [arXiv:1111.3761](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.3761)

**Harmanec P. et al. 2002**
:   *Properties and nature of Be stars. XXI. The long-term and the orbital variations of V832 Cyg = 59 Cyg*, A&A 387, 580.

**Harmanec P. et al. 2025**
